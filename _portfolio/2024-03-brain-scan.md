**Duration:** March 2024  
**Project page:** [konstantinosmitsides.github.io/projects/GNByN](https://konstantinosmitsides.github.io/projects/GNByN/)

Group project of five as part of the MSc in AI at Imperial College London.

- Devised a novel graph neural network-based model to generate higher-resolution brain scan images from their low-resolution counterpart
- Constructed custom graph neural network layers and architecture within PyTorch and PyTorch Geometric
- Trained various models with different hyperparameter settings using GPU clusters
- Finished 16th against 30+ groups in a Kaggle-like competition for the best-performing model

**Tools:** Python, PyTorch, PyTorch Geometric, NetworkX, Slurm
